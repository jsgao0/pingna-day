Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 2025-11-06

Thank you for choosing Pingna Day. For online purchases, we provide a 7-day cooling-off period (counted from the day after receipt; this is not a trial period). You may request a return if the item remains unopened and in resalable condition.

1. Eligibility

  • Request window: within 7 calendar days (Taiwan Time) from the day after receipt.
  • Item condition: unopened, unused, and in original packaging (outer box, inserts, seals, gifts, invoice/packing slip).
  • Order details: please provide order number, recipient name, contact phone, description, and photos (for defects/damages).

2. Non-returnable Items

  • Food/beverage items that have been opened (unless defective).
  • Requests submitted after the 7-day window.
  • Damage caused by improper storage, use, or transportation after delivery (e.g., dents, moisture, swelling).
  • Missing gifts/components or incomplete packaging for sets/bundles.
  • Other cases exempt from the cooling-off right under applicable laws.

3. Defective/Damaged or Wrong Item

If your order arrives defective, damaged, short-shipped, or incorrect, please contact us within 7 days with photos. We will arrange a replacement or return promptly.

  • We bear the shipping cost for returns/exchanges due to our error or product defects.
  • Replacements are processed for the same item; if out of stock, a refund will be issued.

4. Returns for Personal Reasons (within 7 days)

  • For reasons such as taste preference or change of mind, please keep items intact and apply within the time limit.
  • Two-way shipping costs are borne by the customer (subject to actual logistics fees).

5. How to Apply

  1. Submit a request: email customer service with order details and photos (if defective/damaged).
  2. Review & instructions: we reply within 1–2 business days with pickup/return info.
  3. Return packing: pack properly to avoid secondary damage; follow pickup schedule if arranged by us.
  4. Inspection & processing: 1–3 business days upon arrival; we then process replacement or refund.

6. Refunds

  • Refunds are made to the original payment method.
  • Posting time: approximately 3–14 business days after our refund action (varies by bank/issuer).
  • For partial returns, applied discounts/gifts/shipping thresholds may be recalculated.

7. Exchanges

  • Exchanges are limited to the same item; if unavailable, we will issue a refund.
  • For defective/wrong items, we cover one round of shipping.

8. Shipping & Risk

  • For non-defect returns, customers bear return shipping and transit risk; we recommend a trackable service.
  • If the returned item does not meet the conditions, it will be sent back (shipping at customer’s expense).

9. Invoice & Gifts

  • Please return the invoice (or follow e-invoice procedures as applicable).
  • Sets/bundles must be complete; missing items may affect the refund.
