Our Story

Terroir Shapes Pingna Day

Over 200 years ago, tea farmers from Anxi, Fujian settled in Pinglin, bringing centuries of tea-making expertise. Combined with Pinglin's unique climate and geography, they cultivated exceptional tea renowned worldwide.

In Pinglin, every home is a tea factory. "Honoring tradition, embracing innovation" — we bring Pinglin's unique terroir to the world. Come visit Pinglin and experience what we call "Pingna Day."
