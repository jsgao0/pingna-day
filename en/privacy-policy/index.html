Pingna Day Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-11-06

Pingna Day (“the Company”) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. Without your consent, we will not provide your information to any unrelated third party.

Please keep your account and password confidential. After using our services, remember to log out. If you use a shared or public computer, close the browser window to ensure your data security.

1. Collection, Processing, and Use of Personal Data

Purpose of Collection

  • Marketing (Code 040)
  • Customer management and services (Code 090)
  • Agricultural product transactions (Code 138)
  • Online shopping and e-commerce services (Code 148)
  • Statistical and research analysis (Code 157)

Categories of Data

  • C001 Individual identification: name, address, phone, email, etc.
  • C002 Financial identification: credit card or bank account information.
  • C011 Personal description: gender, date of birth, etc.
  • C031 Residence information: address, period of use or residence, etc.
  • C036 Lifestyle: product and service preferences.

Period, Area, Recipients, and Methods

  • Period: From registration until you request termination or we cease services.
  • Area: Taiwan (for international operations: Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore).
  • Recipients & Methods:
    • a. Display member information upon login for personalized services.
    • b. Transactions: delivery, payment, after-sales service, and inquiry responses.
    • c. Marketing & promotions: newsletters, event notices, product recommendations, personalized content.
    • d. Customer service: responses via email, phone, or other channels.
    • e. Related services: operations necessary to achieve the above purposes.
    • f. Partners: share data within necessary scope with logistics, payment, or marketing partners under privacy obligations.

2. Your Rights

Subject to the Personal Data Protection Act, you may:

  • Access or request a copy of your data
  • Request to supplement or correct your data
  • Request to stop collection, processing, or use
  • Request deletion

To exercise these rights, contact our customer service. Refusing to provide necessary information may limit certain services.

3. Data Security

We adopt reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or other risks.

4. Inquiry or Correction

You may contact us by email to inquire about, correct, or delete your personal data. We will process requests promptly in accordance with applicable laws.

5. Cookies

We may use cookies to provide a better and more personalized experience. You may disable or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not function properly.

6. Amendments

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page. We recommend reviewing it periodically.
