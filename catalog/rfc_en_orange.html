RFC 2025 — Sparkling Orange Scented Tea

1. Product Introduction

Following Sparkling Paochong Tea, we introduced Sparkling Orange Scented Tea for those who prefer a sweeter profile or are sensitive to carbonation’s slight acidity. Inspired by Pinglin’s early citrus‑growing heritage, bright orange notes add natural sweet‑tart balance and enhance structure, harmonizing with the tea and bubbles. It honors local terroir while meeting modern tastes—like stepping into the colorful universe of Pinglin in every sip.
